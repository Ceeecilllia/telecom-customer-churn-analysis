# Metric definitions

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total customers | Distinct `customer_id` records in the snapshot |
| Churned customers | Customers whose `customer_status = 'Churned'` |
| Churn rate | Churned customers divided by all customers in the snapshot |
| Monthly revenue exposure | Sum of `monthly_charge_raw` for churned customers; this is a revenue-at-risk proxy, not realized future loss |
| Historical churned revenue | Sum of `total_revenue` historically generated by churned customers |
| Average churned tenure | Mean tenure in months among churned customers |
| Segment churn rate | Churned customers in a segment divided by all customers in that segment |

## Interpretation guardrails

- The dataset is a cross-sectional sample from a fictional company; it does not establish causal effects.
- A segment-level difference can be influenced by other customer characteristics.
- `Total Revenue` is historical revenue and should not be described as revenue lost.
- `Monthly Charge` contains 120 negative records. Raw and validated versions are retained separately.
- Recommended actions should be tested with controlled experiments where possible.
